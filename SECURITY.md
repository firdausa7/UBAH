# Reporting a security problem

Ubah holds sensitive health and safeguarding information about girls and young women. If you find a
security problem, please tell us privately first: do not open a public issue, and do not test it on
the live service or with anyone's real data.

Email: [security contact, e.g. security@your-organisation.org] with what you found, how to reproduce
it, and how we can reach you. We will reply within [5 working days], keep you informed while we fix
it, and credit you if you wish once it is fixed.

In scope: this repository's code and its documented deployment. Out of scope: the outside services
Ubah uses (report those to them), and social engineering of staff.
