Period pain is common, especially in the first days of bleeding. Rest, a warm cloth on your belly, and a painkiller such as paracetamol (follow the packet) can help.
See a nurse if the pain stops you from walking or going to school, if bleeding soaks a pad every hour, or if you also have a fever.
