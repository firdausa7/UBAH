## Ubah and your privacy

**Ubah is a private place to ask about your body and health.** It is run by HPI-SOLUTIONS KE LIMITED with [health partner] and [protection partner].

**We don't ask your name.** You don't need an account. Staff see a short code like "A19F", not your name.

**Your phone number is locked.** We keep it scrambled and locked so we can recognise you and send you a message 3 days after a clinic visit. No one at a clinic sees it.

**What you write stays private.** Your messages are read by Ubah's computer, and by a trained Dada or nurse when you ask for a person or when you may be in danger. They keep it confidential.

**If you may be in danger, a person is told at once.** This is to keep you safe. [Explain what happens next, as agreed with the protection partner — for example, whether anyone outside Ubah is told, and when.]

**Clinics see only your pass code** and the help you need. Never your name or number.

**Your answers about clinics are counted together.** We only show a clinic's score when 5 or more girls have answered, so no one knows what you said.

**Voice notes:** we turn them into text, then delete the recording.

**Your location:** if you share it, we use it once to find a clinic near you, then forget it.

**You are in control:**
- Send **STOP** — no more messages from Ubah.
- Send **DELETE** — we delete your messages, passes and answers.
- In the Ubah app: **Wipe my history**, and **Quick exit** to leave at once.
- Lock the Ubah app with a **PIN** so others can't open it.

**Your rights:** you can ask to see, correct or delete your information. Ask a Dada, or contact [name / phone / email]. You can also complain to Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner: [contact details — check the current ones].

**Who helps us run Ubah:** WhatsApp (Meta), Africa's Talking (SMS), [hosting company], [other providers]. They may store messages to deliver them. [Say where data is stored.]
