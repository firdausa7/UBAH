# Ubah safeguarding procedure

> **DRAFT.** A structure based on how the Ubah software works. It must be written in full with Ubah's health and protection partners, agreed with each site's protection actors, approved by the ethics committee, and trained before any girl uses Ubah. Every name, number, organisation and timeframe in [square brackets] is for partners to fill in — **do not use any contact or hotline number until it has been confirmed with the organisation and marked "Verified" in Ubah (Admin → Sites).**

## 1. Principles
- The girl's safety and wishes come first. Do no harm.
- Confidentiality, with its limits explained to her (see the privacy notice).
- Never blame. Never investigate yourself: refer to trained protection actors.
- Danger signs always reach a trained human; the computer never handles them alone.

## 3. How Ubah raises a case (what the software does)
1. Every message is checked for danger signs (medical emergency, violence/abuse, self-harm) — on every channel and at every step.
2. A danger sign opens an **urgent case**: medical and self-harm go to nurses, safety to Dadas (any case can be escalated to a nurse with one button). Nurses on the alert list get an SMS at once.
3. The girl gets an immediate safety message (approved text) and, for medical danger, an urgent referral pass.
4. If no one replies within **15 minutes**, the nurses are alerted again every 15 minutes until someone replies.
5. Every view, reply, note and step is recorded in the case history and the audit log.

## 4. Responding (for Dadas and nurses)
1. **Open the case within [5] minutes of an alert. Claim it** so others know it's handled.
2. Read the conversation. Reply using simple, warm words; check the AI draft before sending — never send it unchanged without reading.
3. **Medical danger** (heavy bleeding, pregnancy danger signs, fainting): tell her to go to the clinic now with her pass; nurse calls/messages the clinic [process]; ask whether someone can go with her.
4. **Violence or abuse**: set the girl's site on the case and follow the **site checklist** (Admin → Sites → Protection pathway), ticking each step. Offer the agreed referral. Respect her choice unless [mandatory reporting conditions — confirm with legal adviser and partners, especially for under-18s].
5. **Self-harm**: [assessment questions and steps from the health partner]; nurse leads; stay in contact; agree a safe follow-up.
6. Write **internal notes** of what you did — facts only, never her name or number.
7. **Escalate** to a nurse if you are unsure, at any time.
8. Mark the case **handled** only when the agreed steps are done.

## 6. Concerns about staff
- Any concern that a Dada, nurse or other staff member has harmed or exploited a girl, or misused information: report to the safeguarding lead at once ([phone]). The admin deactivates the account immediately pending review (Admin → Staff → Deactivate) and preserves the audit log.
- Staff must never contact a girl outside Ubah, ask for personal details or meet her privately.

## 7. Training and supervision
- Before starting: safeguarding, confidentiality, psychological first aid, the referral pathway for their site, Ubah inbox use. [Provider, hours.]
- Weekly supervision of Dadas by a nurse; debriefs after difficult cases; support for staff wellbeing.

## 8. Records and review
- Monthly: safeguarding lead reviews urgent cases, response times (Dashboard → Urgent handoffs) and the reminder log, and a sample of the audit log.
- After any serious incident: a review within [7] days; changes to this procedure recorded.
